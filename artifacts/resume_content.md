*   **AI Factual Grounding & Citations**: Implemented **LLM Grounding** techniques and automated citation tracking to ensure all AI-generated audit insights are traceable to specific source pages.
*   **Advanced Orchestration**: Leveraged **LangChain** for stateful orchestration, enabling iterative reasoning and complex multi-step audit workflows.
